# Icon attribution

The application icon is derived from an **acoustic guitar** illustration released
into the **public domain** (Creative Commons **CC0 1.0**).

- **Source:** Open Clip Art Library — "Acoustic guitar" by *narrowhouse* (2008)
- **Retrieved via:** Wikimedia Commons, `File:Acoustic_guitar.svg`
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication (no rights reserved)

The original guitar artwork was recoloured as a white silhouette and composited
onto a purple rounded-square background to form the app icon. The original SVG is
kept at `assets/guitar.svg`; `scripts/make_icon.py` regenerates `icon.png` and
`icon.ico` from it.

The **YARG** logo is a trademark of YARC and is intentionally **not** used here.
